### Highlights

- Passion Economy is an economy where people can convert what they do into their source of income
- It’s mostly a content creator economy for now. But will become much broader. Already, Etsy is a part of the passion economy
- Platforms allow more people to do this. Traditional employment is a bundle of different things (means of production, team and camaraderie, insurance, distribution etc). These are now being unbundled and rebundled
- Advertising and subscriptions are both possibilities. Advertising is democratising, and also is a good idea when you’re first starting out. Creating subscription content is a much heavier lift
- Digital communities: one-way vs two-way communication. Community is wildly overused because everyone wants to show network effects. In a community, members should have a strong connection with each other
