# Peter Dodd on Text Analytics (Complexity)

*Podcasts · Technology* · [Source](https://podcasts.apple.com/sg/podcast/complexity/id1482984603?i=1000500318909)

Very similar techniques to the ones I’ve been developing for analysing political discourse, for Nalin’s book and Sanjeev’s PhD!

Hedonometer and Story Wrangler are both interesting projects to check out

[The Hedonometer](https://hedonometer.org/timeseries/en_all/)

[StoryWrangler - Twitter Ngram Search](https://storywrangling.org/?ngrams=dinosaurs,complex%20systems,space\&start=2014-12-09\&end=2019-01-25)

Some interesting ideas:

- Can look at both word usage, and how much words have been amplified over time. Difference in usage and amplification can present an interesting story
- Adding words that have suddenly emerged alongside other words can be important signals. For example, news was a very stable word for decades. Then suddenly, fake started to be associated with it
- You really care about “big movers”. Things that are a high absolute number but have suddenly increased in usage
- Word frequencies and semantic topic frequencies can be a useful predictor of geopolitical risk and macroeconomic risk

Metrics to look at:

- Story turbulence
- Narrative control
- Collective chronopathy — which stories are receiving a greater share of attention over time
