### Highlights

#### Idea 1

**Good On You**: Sustainability rating platform for fashion brands

Business model: get paid by brands and retailers for a rating

Emma Watson is on board and acts as a model

They are both a data aggregator and a collector

#### Idea 2

**Mys Tyler**: Women are unsatisfied with the fit of their clothing when they shop online. Their FIT algorithm finds clothes with the right fit for women by looking at what fits women that have similar bodies (Find your celebrity body double!)

28,000 app installs at a CAC of $0.56

Currenly seeking $1M in seed capital

Monetization: Commissions from brands (10-15%) when someone buys from them, as well as paid partnerships to help brands grow their audience on Instagram

#### Idea 3

**Vpply**: Apply for jobs with video

Assumption: Video applications allow individuals' personality and passion to shine AND immediate first impact from video will help identify whether there's a cultural fit

Seeking $300k in funding for 15% of company

Sales cycle is currently based on personal connections. Don't currently have much of a cycle beyond that

#### Idea 4

**RightPaw**: marketplace that connects breeders and pet buyers, with trust and safety built in

Assumption: Can build into downstream products and services. Breeders and Shelters market is $7M. General dog-care market is $1.4B

3 Revenue Streams:

- Breeder CRM (Monthly subscription)
- Marketplace fees (5-8% commission)
- Affiliate for products & services

Raising $500k right now

#### Idea 5

**Skilligi**: Online classes for kids with a focus on soft skills

Currently have $4k MRR. Aim is to have 2500 subscriptions in 18 months to do MRR of $200k

Rasing seed round of $600k for product dev and marketing

Who does content creation? Will outsource it or use free resources at the start

Ideal customers: families where both parents are working parents

#### Idea 6

**sana.live**: make it easier for your parents (old people) to do more exercise

Personal Health Assistant/Wearable for old people (basically an Alexa skill that connects to your fitness tracker)

#### Idea 7

**MyWhanau**: Family Calendar Assistant App. Basically a calendar that helps you track happenings and key dates for your kids

They ask schools to pay for this. Each school pays $7k/year for this

#### Idea 8

**Activeplace**: Health-focused social network

Stage 1: Purely content based
Stage 2: Virtual & IRL events + insurance

#### Idea 9

Larki: Exceptionally detailed 3-d surveys for architecture

Know the world in high-res 3d. You can measure, navigate, adn crop

There are only 100 site data points in paper. Larki has 30 million poins in 3d

$2M of leads in sales pipeline \[?]. Growing at 68% revenue. Most revenue right now is one-off revenue from data. But hope to add more recurring platform revenue in the future

How do you actually do the 3-d modelling?

- Planes flying over, cars driving down the road, drones. Aggregating everything

#### Idea 10

M8buy: Social buying layer integrated on any ecommerce store (basically Pinduoduo for non-China markets)

- Invite friends to form a shopping group
- Each friend has 24-48 hours to make their order
- As more friends buy, each group member gets an instant "cash back" reward when the time is up

It helps retaiers sell far more

- Existing customers bring new customers
- Buy now, not later
- Only pay a commission for guaranteed sales

Currently doing their first pilot

$177M ARR opportunity

Looking for $250k to facilitate growth
