### Highlights

- Forklifting of time into your airpods keeps increasing
- For a lot of people, the default podcast player is a video app
- Audio has different modes – passive (you just want to chill and listen to other people) vs active (you want to take notes or talk) modes

Augmented Audio: can you bring more layers into audio

- Audio today is a very one-way consumption experience. Can this be a two way experience?
- What tools does the creator want? What does the creator want to hear from his fans?
- Remixing and annotating audio

Recommendations

- People's music listening predicts their podcast tastes
