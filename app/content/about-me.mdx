# About Me

## Operating Principles

1. **Always add more value than you extract**: Never indulge in rent-seeking behaviour, and always add more value than you extract from the friends, colleagues, and partners you spend time with
2. **Spend your time doing things that are meaningful:** You’ve hated yourself when you’ve been doing mundane, run-of-the-mill things that a generic smart person can do. Spend your time and energy on things that have a massive impact and that you are uniquely positioned to do
3. **Prioritize long-term sustainability, not short-term cash grabs**: Always prioritize what is important for the long-term, instead of the flavor of the month
4. **Have Fun**: If you wake up not looking forward to your day for too many days in a row, it’s time to hit the reset button on whatever it is you are doing. This applies to work, relationships, and side-projects
5. **Do not let success in any of work, play, or relationships be an excuse for failure in another area: T**here is little point in having what seems like a great personal life, but not progressing much in your creative and technical endeavours – you will become antsy after a while. Conversly, there is little point in being extremely productive at work, but having a miserable personal life – you will burn out quickly and feel like shit in the long term

## Personal Chronology

### 2005

- Got a scholarship to move from Delhi to Singapore

### 2009

- Built my first business in the final year of high school – an online t-shirt company for my high school + a flower delivery service for valentine's day, generated $40,000 in revenue
- Led a student club of 400 students
- Represented Singapore in a students' entrepreneurship "world cup" in Brasil

![](/notion/about-me/untitled.png)

### 2014

- Launched an edtech company in my final year of college. We did adaptive math learning for students. This eventually failed, because I foolishly went for a B2B model without having an idea of how to sell to businesses

![](/notion/about-me/untitled-1.png)

### 2015

Launched a news company that featured [best-in-class technology](https://medium.com/@rishdotblog/reimagining-news-for-the-web-part-2-565bd617020e) around building CMSes, doing research, and personalizing offerings for users. Quickly realized that news was a bad business to be in and shut down the company, but the core technology developed here helped us generate hundreds of thousands of dollars in revenue over the next 5 years (with Loki.ai), and remains a major part of Data Narratives today

![](/notion/about-me/untitled-2.png)

### 2016

Worked as a freelance data products developer around Asia. Helped companies in Singapore, the UAE, and India create dashboards, do research, and build predictive models.

Got to fly around a fair bit on tickets paid for by clients, which (as a 24 year old) was awesome!

### 2017 - 2021: Loki.ai

Launched a bootstrapped data APIs and insights company, and generated consistent 6-figure annual revenues over 4 years. Used that to feed myself, continue experimenting with tech, and learn how to sell and market my products.

Also won a few hackathons and traveled around Asia and Europe. Sold APIs and dashboards to media companies, universities, FMCG companies, political analysts, and government departments – and began to understand emerging data use-cases that existing BI tools weren't solving.

### 2020: Popper.ai

As GPT-3 and other language models became the rage, we experimented with these and created an automated news media product, which was [wildly successful](https://twitter.com/rishdotblog/status/1368526129477296135)! I realized that we could attack a *much* larger market if we took our insights mining and report generation technology, and made it productizable beyond the media industry. So we winded down our media arm, and began to conceptualize a general purpose solution for data research

### 2021 - 2022: Data Narratives

We build a search engine to access data around the world, and sold it to media companies, public policy teams and think tanks. This was well on its way to grow into a highly profitable lifestyle business, but we realized that the market for it would be small. So we pivoted to Defog.ai.

### 2023 -

Defog is a culmination of everything that I've built over the years, converted to a primarily [self-serve SaaS product](https://defog.ai). I have never been as excited to work on a project, and have deep conviction that it will change the way data analysis is done around the world.
